From hive to hand, five recorded steps

Each stage writes an immutable entry to the ledger, so no step in the journey can be quietly skipped or altered.

Registered hive

Beekeeper and hive are enrolled with a digital ID and GPS location.

Harvest logged

Yield, date and hive sensor history are attached to a new batch record.

Lab & quality check

Purity and moisture results are hashed and written to the chain.

Sealed & QR-labelled

Bottling generates a unique QR tied permanently to the batch record.

Consumer scans

Anyone can verify origin, quality and the beekeeper behind the jar.

One platform, three layers working together

Blockchain secures the record, IoT and AI keep the hives healthy and productive, and a simple scan brings both to the consumer.

Blockchain batch ledger

Every harvest, lab result and ownership transfer is written to an immutable ledger, creating a single source of truth from hive to shelf.

Consumer QR verification

A scan on any phone shows the beekeeper, harvest date and quality results — no app install required.

IoT hive monitoring

Temperature, humidity, weight and acoustic sensors inside each hive stream live readings to the beekeeper's dashboard.

AI disease & yield prediction

Models trained on sensor and imagery data flag early signs of colony stress and forecast expected honey yield per hive.

Built to scale across beekeeping clusters

A deployment path designed around how KVIC and its state boards already organise beekeepers, not around replacing it.

  • 1

    Cluster onboarding

    Local Honey Mission coordinators register beekeepers and hives in bulk through a field app, in local languages.

  • 2

    Shared sensor kits

    Low-cost IoT kits are rotated across hives within a cluster, keeping hardware cost per beekeeper low.

  • 3

    Regional lab network

    Existing KVIC-affiliated testing labs upload purity results directly into the ledger.

  • 4

    Market linkage

    Verified batches become eligible for listing with aggregators and retailers seeking traceable honey.
